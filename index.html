Your Tidal playlist,as a Bandcamp shopping list

Sign in with Tidal, pick a playlist, and every track gets a one-tap Bandcamp search. Tick tracks off as you buy them; your progress stays on this device.

One-time setup

  1. Sign in at developer.tidal.com and create an app.
  2. In the app settings, add this exact redirect URI:
  3. Turn on the scopes user.read, playlists.read, collection.read, search.read and playlists.write. The last two let the page match an uploaded list on Tidal and save it as a playlist.
  4. Copy the app's client ID and paste it here. The client secret isn't needed.

Connect your Tidal account

You'll sign in on Tidal's own page. This site only gets read access to your playlists.
